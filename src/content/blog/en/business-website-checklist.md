A business website isn't an online brochure. It's the first employee who talks to your customers, around the clock. Here's the list we check on every project.

## The foundation

- **Your own domain and email on that domain** (not @gmail.com).
- **SSL certificate** and correctly configured DNS.
- **CDN hosting**, so the site is fast everywhere.

## The experience

- **Mobile-first**: most visitors arrive on a phone.
- **Speed**: content in under two seconds, optimised images.
- **Accessibility**: good contrast, alt text, keyboard navigation.
- **Dark mode**, for those who prefer it.

## Visibility

- **Technical SEO**: titles, descriptions, XML sitemap, robots.txt.
- **AEO**: an FAQ page with direct answers.
- **GEO**: an `llms.txt` file and consistent information everywhere.
- **OpenGraph images**, so links look good when shared.
- **Google Business Profile** filled in and reviews visible on the site.

## Contact

- **A form with spam protection**.
- **Click-to-call** and a **WhatsApp button** on mobile.
- **Google Maps** if you have a physical location.

## Legal and tracking

- **Terms and conditions** and a **privacy policy**.
- **Cookie consent** in line with the GDPR.
- **Google Analytics**, plus Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel if you run ads.

## After launch

- **Maintenance** and backups.
- **Monthly reports** on what works and what doesn't.
- **A partner** who picks up the phone.

If your site ticks fewer than half of these boxes, it's time for a conversation.
